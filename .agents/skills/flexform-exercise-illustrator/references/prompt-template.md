# Prompt template

Use this structure for each independent exercise asset. Replace every bracketed field with movement-specific facts.

```text
Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: dedicated FlexForm exercise hero illustration for a mobile exercise library
Input images: Image 1 is the master visual reference and character/style anchor only; do not crop or edit it
Primary request: Create an original [EXERCISE NAME] exercise demonstration
Scene/backdrop: pure white background with generous whitespace and only subtle grounding shadows
Subject: the same athletic adult male shown in the master reference—warm medium skin, short dark hair, muscular but believable proportions, black athletic shorts and black training shoes
Style/medium: the master reference's polished semi-realistic fitness infographic rendering, anatomy definition, line work, shading, and equipment treatment
Composition/framing: horizontal composition inside a square canvas. LEFT shows [START POSITION]. CENTER shows one simple dark navy directional arrow. RIGHT shows [FINISH POSITION]. Keep both athletes and all equipment fully visible.
Anatomy strip: below the main demonstration, include two small neutral-gray anatomical figures, front and back. Strongly highlight [PRIMARY MUSCLES] in [ACCENT COLOR]. Lightly highlight [SECONDARY MUSCLES] in a softer tint. Keep every unrelated muscle neutral.
Movement accuracy: [GRIP, JOINT, EQUIPMENT, AND PATH CONSTRAINTS]
Constraints: one consistent character in every pose; correct anatomy and equipment; clean mobile-card readability; no title; no labels; no letters; no numbers; no exercise instructions; no random text; no borders; no logos; no watermark
Avoid: [KNOWN CONFUSIONS OR UNSAFE FORM]
```

## Bench-press example

```text
Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: dedicated FlexForm exercise hero illustration for a mobile exercise library
Input images: Image 1 is the master visual reference and character/style anchor only; do not crop or edit it
Primary request: Create an original BARBELL BENCH PRESS exercise demonstration
Scene/backdrop: pure white background with generous whitespace and only subtle grounding shadows
Subject: the same athletic adult male shown in the master reference—warm medium skin, short dark hair, muscular but believable proportions, black athletic shorts and black training shoes
Style/medium: the master reference's polished semi-realistic fitness infographic rendering, anatomy definition, line work, shading, and equipment treatment
Composition/framing: horizontal composition inside a square canvas. LEFT shows the START POSITION: athlete lying on a flat bench holding a loaded Olympic barbell above the chest with arms extended. CENTER shows one simple dark navy downward movement arrow. RIGHT shows the FINISH/BOTTOM POSITION: barbell lowered under control toward mid-chest with forearms near vertical, feet planted, shoulders retracted, and hips on the bench. Keep both athletes, barbell, plates, bench, and rack fully visible.
Anatomy strip: below the main demonstration, include two small neutral-gray anatomical figures, front and back. Strongly highlight pectoralis major in blue. Lightly highlight anterior deltoids and triceps in a softer blue. Keep every unrelated muscle neutral.
Movement accuracy: medium-width symmetrical grip; straight bar; matched plates; bar travels above mid-chest; wrists stacked above elbows; safe rack geometry
Constraints: one consistent character in every pose; correct anatomy and equipment; clean mobile-card readability; no title; no labels; no letters; no numbers; no exercise instructions; no random text; no borders; no logos; no watermark
Avoid: incline bench, decline bench, floating bar, mismatched plates, bent bar, elbows directly flared to 90 degrees, bar behind the head, malformed hands or rack
```
